Privacy Policy — Property Vault

Effective date: November 25, 2026
Applies to the mobile application Property Vault for iPhone (iOS) and Android (App Store bundle ID and Google Play package name: com.ehi.propertyvault).

Property Vault is an app for keeping track of your real estate and related finances. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is protected. The short version: your data stays on your device. We do not collect it, we do not see it, and we do not share or sell it.

1. Data stored on your device only

Everything you enter in Property Vault — properties, agreements, payments, income records, comments, attached documents and photos — is stored locally on your device in the app's private storage. This information is never transmitted to us or to any server we operate. We operate no servers and have no access to your data.

Like other apps, the app's private storage may be included in the backups your phone makes to your own iCloud (iPhone) or Google (Android) account, if device backup is turned on in your phone's settings. Those backups are controlled by you and by Apple or Google, not by us.

2. App account

The app asks you to create a local profile (name/e-mail and password) to separate data between users of the same device. These credentials are stored only on your device. They are not sent anywhere and cannot be recovered by us. No online account is created.

3. Biometric unlock

If you enable Face ID or Touch ID (iPhone) or fingerprint or face unlock (Android), the check is performed entirely by your device's operating system. The app never sees, stores, or transmits your biometric data.

4. Camera and photos

The app uses the camera or the system photo picker only when you choose to attach a photo or document to a property. Selected images are copied into the app's private storage on your device. The app does not scan your photo library. On Android it does not request media library permissions. On iPhone, the system may ask for permission to access or add to your photo library; this is used only when you choose to attach a photo or to save an exported document or photo to your library.

5. Purchases (Pro subscription)

Subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store (on iPhone) or Google Play Billing (on Android). We never receive your payment details. To validate purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated anonymous identifier and purchase information (product, price, purchase state). It does not receive your name, e-mail, or any of the data you store in the app. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

6. Currency exchange rates

To convert between currencies, the app requests current exchange rates from the public Frankfurter API. Only the currency codes you use (for example, EUR, USD) are included in the request — no personal data.

7. Backups you create

You can export your data as a backup file (JSON or ZIP) to a location you choose. Backup files are created only on your request and are under your control. Keep them safe: anyone with access to a backup file can read the data inside it. Passwords are not included in backups.

8. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking

The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no trackers. We do not collect usage statistics, crash reports, device identifiers, or location data.

9. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual records inside the app at any time, or remove all data for your login with MyAccount → Profile → Delete profile. You can also remove all app data by deleting the app (on iPhone: touch and hold the app icon → Remove App → Delete App; on Android: Settings → Apps → Property Vault → Clear data, or uninstall). Subscriptions are not cancelled by deleting the app: manage them in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on any server. Backup files you exported must be deleted by you from wherever you saved them.

10. Children

Property Vault is a finance tool intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from children.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
